Hazel Chen

Third-year mechanical engineering student at the University of Virginia, minoring in electrical engineering. I focus on developing lithium-ion battery systems and electric vehicle components.

I lead the electrical and tractive battery subteam on the UVA Formula SAE EV team, where I work on the tractive battery and the high-voltage electronics around it. On my own time I build boards and devices, from a film-scanning light source to a pocket sensor kit. I like taking a project all the way from schematic and CAD to a working, tested part.

Work for Virginia Motorsports

Projects I worked on for Virginia Motorsports, UVA's Formula SAE team.

  • Tractive Battery

    128S pouch-cell battery pack for our 2026-27 FSAE EV season.

    Autodesk Fusion

  • Tractive Battery Thermal Model

    MATLAB model predicting cell temperature and state of charge over a full FSAE endurance run.

    MATLAB

  • High Voltage Controller (HVC)

    One board for everything between the tractive battery and the car: precharge, contactors, isoSPI, and safety latches.

    Altium Designer

  • Segment BMS Rev 2

    Two-board cell monitoring stack for each 16-cell segment. I redesigned it for Rev 2, cutting the cell tap board from 6 layers to 2.

    Altium Designer

  • High Voltage Indicator Light

    Tractive-powered isolated supply driving a low-voltage panel-mount light whenever the tractive system is energized.

    Altium Designer

  • Benchtop Power Distribution Board

    Switched, fused, and current-sensed 5 V, 3.3 V, and input rails for bench-testing custom PCBs.

    Altium Designer

  • Fusible Link Simulation

    Coupled electro-thermal Ansys model of nickel-strip fusible links for a cylindrical-cell pack.

    Ansys MechanicalWorkbench
